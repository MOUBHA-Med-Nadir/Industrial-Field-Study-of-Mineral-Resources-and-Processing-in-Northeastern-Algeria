Industrial Field Study of Mineral Resources and Processing in Northeastern Algeria
Overview
This repository documents an industrial field study conducted as part of the Master's program in Mineral Resources, Geomaterials, and Environment (RMGE) at the University of Mohammed Seddik Ben Yahia – Jijel. The five-day intensive fieldwork (October 2024) provided direct observation of extraction, processing, and production procedures for essential natural resources used in construction, ceramics, and glass manufacturing.

Study Context
The field study aimed to bridge theoretical knowledge with industrial practice by visiting key mineral processing facilities in northeastern Algeria. Students observed:

Cement production processes

Aggregate extraction and processing

Kaolin mining and beneficiation

Glass manufacturing from silica sand

Safety and environmental monitoring systems

I. Hamma Bouziane Cement Plant (Constantine)
Location
Address: National Road N°3, 12 km from Constantine, 7 km from Hamma Bouziane

Altitude: 525 m

Area: 129 hectares

Owner: Groupe Industriel des Ciments d'Algérie (GICA)

Production Capacity (as of October 22, 2024)
Product	Daily Production	Monthly Production	Annual Production
Clinker	~2,800 t/day	48,547.57 t (107.88% of target)	669,688.54 t (89.53% of target)
Cement	3,071.67 t/day	51,093.33 t (85.16% of target)	576,790 t (72.10% of target)
Cement Manufacturing Process
Raw Materials
Limestone: ~80% (provides CaO)

Clay/Marl: ~20% (provides SiO₂, Al₂O₃, Fe₂O₃)

Gypsum: ~5% (added during grinding to regulate setting)

Production Stages
Stage	Description
1. Extraction & Crushing	Limestone and marl extracted from quarries; crushed to 0-25 mm
2. Raw Meal Preparation	Formulation, pre-homogenization, grinding, and homogenization
3. Preheating	Raw meal heated in cyclone preheater tower to remove moisture
4. Pre-calcination	Partial decarbonation at 850-900°C (CO₂ released from CaCO₃)
5. Clinkerization	Rotary kiln at 1,450°C – formation of clinker nodules
6. Cement Grinding	Clinker ground with gypsum and additives
7. Storage & Dispatch	Silos (2,000-5,000 t capacity); dispatched in bulk or 50 kg bags
Quality Control
Analysis Type	Parameters	Equipment
Chemical	CaO (60-70%), SiO₂ (~20%), Al₂O₃ (3-8%), Fe₂O₃ (0.5-6%), MgO (<5%), SO₃ (<3%)	XRF (X-ray Fluorescence)
Physical	Fineness (3,000-4,000 cm²/g Blaine), Setting time (initial 30-45 min, final <10h), Density (3.15 g/cm³)	Blaine apparatus, Vicat apparatus, Le Chatelier apparatus
Mechanical	Compressive strength (≥42.5 MPa at 28 days)	Compression testing press
Mineralogical	Clinker phases	XRD, SEM
Concrete Proportions (Ordinary Concrete)
Component	Percentage
Cement	10-15%
Sand	25-30%
Gravel	40-50%
Water	10-15%
II. Chekfa Quarry (SARL NOSTRAP Brighen)
Location
Site: Al-M'cif, 4 km northwest of Chekfa, southeast of Jijel

Boundaries: South – Oued Aftis; West – Mechta El Massiat; East – Mechta El Adjarda; North – Dechra Toûsna

Geological Facies
Formation	Description
Kabyle Basement	Gneiss, micaschists, schists – grayish color, foliated, low hardness
Metamorphosed Limestone (Marble)	Grayish/reddish strata (iron oxide staining); reacts with HCl (effervescence); contains calcite
Skarn	Greenish traces observed with HCl – indicates contact metamorphism
Extraction Process
1. Drilling
Equipment: Junjin JD-800 hydraulic rotary-percussion drill

Hole Diameter: 89 mm

Drill Rod Length: 3 m

Drilling Depth: 13 m

Orientation: Vertical or sub-vertical

2. Blasting
Detonating Cord: PETN core (12 g/m)

Detonators:

DEI (Instantaneous) – initiates detonation chain

DMR (Micro-delay) – 20 ms intervals between numbers for controlled fragmentation

Explosives: Selected based on rock density and resistance

3. Loading & Haulage
Equipment: Hydraulic excavators, wheel loaders, Shacman dump trucks, bulldozers

Water Trucks: Dust suppression

Aggregate Processing
Stage	Equipment	Output
Primary Crushing	Jaw crusher (1 m opening)	0-300 mm
Primary Screening	Vibrating screen	Separation by size
Secondary Crushing	Impact crusher (250 t/h)	Materials >40 mm reduced
Secondary Screening	Cribles 1 & 2	0-3 mm, 3-8 mm, 8-15 mm, 15-25 mm
Mining Method: Downward bench mining (top to bottom)

III. Tamazert Kaolin Deposit (El Milia, Jijel)
Location
Coordinates: 36°5' N, 6°5' E

Distance: 45 km from Jijel, 10 km from Mediterranean coast, 17 km north of El Milia

Operator: SOALKA (Société des Kaolins d'Algérie)

63% Federal White Cement (Canada)

37% ENOF

Geology
Host Rock: Gneiss, micaschists, granitic gneiss

Origin: In situ weathering of gneiss

Orientation: NE-SW, dipping NW

Deposit Bodies:

Central body (largest reserves)

Northern body

Sidi Kader body (best quality)

Mineralogy
Mineral	Formula	Characteristics
Kaolinite	Al₂Si₂O₅(OH)₄	Main mineral; foliated, pearly/dull luster, plastic
Halloysite	Al₂Si₂O₅(OH)₄·2H₂O	Similar to kaolinite with water layer
Illite	Mica-type clay	Dioctahedral/trioctahedral forms
Quartz	SiO₂	Abundant; veinlets in fractures
Baryte	BaSO₄	White to brown; no economic value
Iron Oxide	Fe₂O₃	Red/brown inclusions; from alteration
Mining Method
Type: Open-pit (selective mining)

Process: Overburden removal → Excavation → Extraction

Quality Criteria: Whiteness, purity (~13%), viscosity, specific surface area, abrasiveness

Beneficiation Process
Stage	Description
Homogenization	Mixing raw kaolin on large platform for uniform quality
Crushing & Screening	Reduction to ≤250 mm; 2-stage screens
Levigation	Cyclone separation of fine particles
Filtration & Decantation	Concentration: F2 (73% wt), F3 (76% wt)
Chemical Bleaching	Iron oxide removal (KT3B – 25% of installed capacity)
Drying	Industrial dryers
Final Products
Kaolin F2 & F3: General industrial applications

Bleached Kaolin (KT3B): High whiteness, high purity applications

Plant Capacity: 50,000 tons/year (commissioned 1994)

IV. Djebel M'rada Quarry (Ziama Mansouria, Jijel)
Location
Coordinates: ~36.6500° N, ~5.4500° E

Access: RN43 coastal road

Operator: ENTPM (Entreprise Nationale des Travaux aux Puits et Mines)

Geology
Dominant Feature: Djebel M'rada – Liassic limestone/dolomitic massif interpreted as a klippe

Triassic Complex: Gypsiferous marls, schists, limestones, dolomites, gabbros

Tectonic Setting: Faults (NW-SE orientation); March 1968 earthquake caused collapse of NE compartment

Production Process
1. Extraction (Drilling & Blasting)
Hydraulic drills

Industrial explosives with vibration control

2. Loading
Hydraulic excavators

Wheel loaders

3. Haulage
Articulated/rigid dump trucks to crushing station

4. Crushing
Capacity: 100-150 tons/hour

Stages: Primary jaw crusher → Secondary impactor/crusher → Screening

5. Final Products
Various aggregate sizes for construction and infrastructure

Dust Reduction Strategies
Water sprayers at crushers, conveyors, and stockpiles

Dust capture systems (filters)

Covered conveyors

Regular watering of access roads

Tree planting/anti-wind barriers

Mandatory dust masks for workers

Air quality monitoring

V. AFRICAVER Glass Factory (Taher, Jijel)
General Information
Founded: 1982 (production started 1987)

Location: Taher industrial zone, 17 km from Jijel

Area: 17 hectares

Employees: ~300

Original Process: Pittsburgh method (20,000 t/year)

Diversification:

Cast glass (April 1994)

Glass bricks (November 1994)

Safety glass (1992) – 44,000 laminated windshields/year

Raw Materials
Primary: Silica sand (from Kef Hadjrate quarry, Aouana region)

Additional: Limestone, dolomite, sodium carbonate

Sand Quality: ~99% SiO₂; rounded/sub-rounded grains; uniform granulometry

Sand Processing
Stage	Description
Crushing & Screening	Rod mill (0-15 mm → 0-1 mm); shaking table for density separation
Washing	Removal of dust, clay, and organic matter
Drying	Steam drying to remove residual moisture
Storage	Silos or warehouses
Glass Production Units
Cast Glass (Printed Glass)
Raw Material	Weight (kg)
Sand	335
Cullet (recycled glass)	200
Sodium Carbonate	100
Dolomite	65
Limestone	45
Sodium Sulfate	4.81
Process: Mixing (2-3 min) → Melting in tank furnace (1,200°C) → Glass paste → Plates

Sodium Silicate
Raw Material	Weight (kg)
Sand	275
Sodium Carbonate	210
Process: Melting (1,200-1,400°C) → Solid sodium silicate → Dissolution in autoclave (140-160°C, 6-8 bar) → Liquid sodium silicate

Applications: Adhesives, detergents, water treatment, refractory products, cements, chemical binders

Safety Glass Products
Type	Description	Applications
Laminated Glass	Two+ glass sheets with PVB interlayer; retains shards upon breakage	Automotive windshields, façades, impact-resistant glazing
Armored Glass	Cut and drilled with diamond discs	Banks, public institutions, high-security buildings
Tempered Glass	Heated to ~620°C, rapidly cooled (air quench); breaks into small non-cutting fragments	Doors, windows, shower cabins, urban furniture
Production: 50 windshields/hour; future: train windshields

Conclusions
This industrial field study provided students with comprehensive exposure to the mineral resource value chain in northeastern Algeria:

Sector	Key Learning
Cement	Full production cycle from quarry to finished product; quality control protocols
Aggregates	Quarry operations, blasting, crushing, screening, and dust management
Kaolin	Open-pit selective mining; beneficiation through levigation, filtration, and bleaching
Glass	Silica sand processing; glass manufacturing techniques (cast, laminated, tempered)
Safety	Industry-standard PPE, surveillance, and blasting management systems
Significance of This Study
Industrial Exposure: Direct observation of real-world mineral processing operations

Process Understanding: Detailed knowledge of cement, aggregate, kaolin, and glass production chains

Quality Control: Familiarity with chemical, physical, mechanical, and mineralogical testing

Safety Awareness: Understanding of occupational health and safety in industrial settings

Career Preparation: Practical skills for mineral resources and geomaterials industry
